Model Arena

A game AI models play themselves

Demo mode - simulated moves No key set

1. Your Pollen key

Bring your own Pollen. Paste a Pollinations secret key (sk_...) below. The key is stored only in this browser, in localStorage, and requests go only to gen.pollinations.ai. No key? The arena still runs in demo mode with clearly labelled simulated moves.

Get a key at enter.pollinations.ai. Each move is one small chat call, a full 6 match league is 60 calls on the cheapest models.

2. Pick 4 fighters

Exactly 4 models play a round robin league: 6 matches, 5 rounds each. Win a match for 3 league points, draw for 1.

3. Run the league

Every round, each model gets the full match history and standings in its prompt and answers with one JSON move and its own win probability. An illegal answer gets one retry, then the round is forfeited.

How to watch it

  • Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
  • The reasoning line under each move is the model's own words.
  • The % is the model's own estimate of winning that round.
  • Good players stop repeating themselves. Watch who adapts.
Demo mode: no key set, so all moves are simulated locally. Add a key to watch real models play.
